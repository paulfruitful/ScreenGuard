# Screen Guard v1.3.3 — Release Notes

**Browse Safe. Live Free.**  
*Release Date: November 25, 2026 | Platform: Android 11–16*

---

## Overview

Screen Guard v1.3.3 strengthens clothed suggestive and social-feed protection by running **on-device ML Kit Pose, OCR, and contextual NLP** across major social video apps, adding censored slang decoding, and introducing a fast on-device directory of **420+ adult accounts, adult studios, and explicit hashtags** — all while preserving strict zero-false-positive verification for normal videos.

---

## What's New in v1.3.3

### Always-On Social Media Verification
- **Ungated Pose, OCR & NLP on Social Video Apps:** X (Twitter), TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat, and Reddit now run lightweight ML Kit Pose, burned-in caption OCR, and contextual NLP even when the subject is fully clothed.
- **Clothed Twerking & Suggestive Posture Detection:** Sustained rear-facing, bent-over, and hip-centered poses accumulate across a 3-frame (~2.2 s) window while allowing gym workouts, sports, and talking-head vlogs.

### Adult Account Directory, Hashtags & Slang Decoding
- **420+ Known Adult Accounts & Studios:** Instant on-device matching for known adult/porn X handles, `@*xxx` handles, and adult studio brands (`Brazzers`, `Chaturbate`, `Fake Hub`, `Reality Kings`, `BangBros`) — matched strictly on `@handle` / URL patterns so ordinary words never false-positive.
- **Explicit Hashtags & Adult Link Trees:** Flags `#nsfw`, `#porn`, `#xxx`, `#twerk`, `#goon`, `#chudai`, `#onlyfans`, `#nudes`, and adult link domains (`onlylinks.com`, `onlyfans.com`, `fansly.com`).
- **Censored & Multilingual Slang Decoder:** Normalizes elongated and censored text (`jrkkk`, `D*ck`, `pxrns`) and covers multilingual explicit/suggestive slang (`wataa`, `chudai`, `goon`, `goonette`, `doggy`, `missionary`, `cowgirl`, `reversecowgirl`, `69`, and more).

### Multi-Modal Zero-False-Positive Foundation
- **ML Kit Face Detection (Talking-Head Veto):** Prevents false shields on close-up vlogs, camisoles, and dim-room portraits.
- **Dual-Model Agreement & 2–3 Frame Accumulative Gate:** Requires Stage 1 + Stage 2 corroboration and multi-frame stability so single-frame spikes never interrupt safe browsing.
- **In-App GitHub Releases OTA Updater:** Automatically checks for new versions and updates in-app with a live progress bar.

## Privacy Guarantee

- **100% On-Device Processing:** Zero cloud uploads and zero external servers.
- **Zero Disk Retention:** Screen frames are inspected in temporary memory and discarded immediately.
- **DNS Privacy:** Local DNS filtering never logs or persists browsing history.
