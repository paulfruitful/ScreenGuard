Overview

Release Highlights

Screen Guard v1.0.0 introduces a faster, stricter, and more reliable system-wide content protection experience designed for everyday users. Explicit photos and videos across social media, browsers, and third-party apps are covered automatically with a calm protective shield, while your screen data never leaves your phone.

Strict Protection Policy

Balanced mode and temporary dismiss options have been removed so protection stays consistent and firm whenever explicit content appears.

Faster Social Feed Detection

Focused video-region analysis detects explicit content in X (Twitter), Instagram, TikTok, and third-party feeds on the very first frame.

Auto-Fade Recovery

As soon as you scroll or switch back to safe content, the protective shield automatically fades away without interrupting your app.

What Is New

Features
  • Scripture Encouragement on the Protective Shield: Whenever the shield covers explicit content, it presents a randomly selected Bible verse ministering against lust and temptation, paired with a Read Full Scripture in Bible button that opens the passage directly in your Bible app or on bible.com.
  • Direct Return to Safe Browsing: Tapping Return to Safe Browsing exits the offending post in the background and takes you straight back to the Screen Guard Home screen.
  • Refreshed Cross-in-Shield Interface: Simplified Home, Activity, Capture, and Settings screens with clear status indicators and plain-language controls for non-technical users.
  • Trusted App Exclusions: Easily exclude messaging or utility apps you trust from Settings.

Reliability & Performance Improvements

Stability
  • Background Service Persistence: Promoted the accessibility observer to a foreground service so Android and Xiaomi HyperOS task managers keep protection active in the background without dropping into a stopped state.
  • Restart Notification on Home Screen: If your phone's battery manager ever stops the background service, the Home screen clearly alerts you with a one-tap link to restore it.
  • Zero-Stall Capture Pipeline: Removed blocking background window queries and stale frame queues so continuous video playback on X, Instagram, and TikTok is inspected smoothly every second.
  • Zero-Retention Privacy Guarantee: All screen checks happen transiently in device memory with no screenshots saved to storage and no audio or images uploaded.

Setup & Compatibility

Android 11+
  • Supported Apps: Verified across X (Twitter), Instagram, TikTok, Google Chrome, YouTube, Facebook, and standard third-party Android applications.
  • System Privacy Respect: Password fields, banking screens, and private/incognito windows protected by Android remain untouched.
